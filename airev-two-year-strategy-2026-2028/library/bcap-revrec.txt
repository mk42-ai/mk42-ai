28 using the equity method.6 The JV is a separate reporting entity and records 100% of its own revenue, costs, assets and liabilities. AIREV does not
line-by-line consolidate 50% of JV revenue or 50% of JV costs — proportionate consolidation is prohibited for joint ventures.6
