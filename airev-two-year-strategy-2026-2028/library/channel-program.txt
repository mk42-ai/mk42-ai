Two settings work in deliberate tension: capacity to rotate, and discipline not to overrun. Four licenses — engineered for rotation Each deployment runs for 30 to 60 days.

Four licenses — engineered for rotation Each deployment runs for 30 to 60 days. Sustaining a cadence of approximately one new client per month across 24 months means multiple trials will routinely be live simultaneously — one concluding while the next two are already underway. Four base licenses provide the concurrency headroom to keep the pipeline in continuous motion, so no qualified prospect waits in a queue for a license to free up. Two licenses would force trials into strict sequence and bottleneck the program; four allow it to flow.

trials into strict sequence and bottleneck the program; four allow it to flow. A twelve-deployment cap — engineered for quality The cap is the counterweight to that headroom. Four licenses could technically support far more than twelve trials, but every deployment is backed by hands-on forward-deployed engineering — and the depth of that support is what converts trials into transactions.

— and the depth of that support is what converts trials into transactions. Capping the program at twelve deployments keeps engineering attention genuinely dedicated per client, protects delivery quality, and requires each trial slot to be allocated to a qualified, high-intent prospect rather than diffused across speculative activity. SECTION 07 Account Protection To protect Redington’s investment of effort in developing each account, exclusivity is granted per account and triggered at registration. From the moment a trial is registered against a named legal entity, that account is exclusive to Redington for the duration of the trial plus six months following deployment.

Redington for the duration of the trial plus six months following deployment. Within that window, AIREV will not run a parallel trial or pursue a direct engagement with the same account through any other channel. Protection is earned, not blanket. It attaches only on formal registration of a trial against a specific named account — never pre-emptively across a target list — and an account showing no engagement activity for a sustained period may be released back into the general pipeline.
