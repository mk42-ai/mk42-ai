11Intel × AIREV

From AI PC to Xeon server

OnDemand co-optimised across the Intel stack — on-device agents on next-gen AI PCs, then Xeon 6 and Gaudi 3 in the data centre.

AIREV and Intel partner to launch fully on-device AI agents on Intel AI PCs. Photograph via GEC Newswire, December 2025. Intel, the Intel logo, Xeon, Gaudi and Intel Core are trademarks of Intel Corporation or its subsidiaries.

Sources: GEC Newswire, Dec 2025 (gecnewswire.com/airev-and-intel-partner-to-launch-fully-on-device-ai-agents/) · TradingView/Reuters 14 Oct 2025 (MOU) · TradingView/Reuters 17 Dec 2025 (on-device agents) · Intel Business / LinkedIn (OnDemand Intel Technical Certification).

  • 14 Oct 2025 — MOU to optimise OnDemand for Intel Xeon and Gaudi, and co-market agentic AI from AI PCs to servers.
  • Xeon 6 — low-latency inference for smaller models (≤10B parameters) inside OnDemand.
  • Gaudi 3 — high-concurrency LLM inference; unified Intel-optimised stack.
  • 17 Dec 2025 — AI agents that run entirely on-device on Intel next-gen AI PCs; 300+ agents/tools; no cloud dependency.
  • AMX — Intel Advanced Matrix Extensions, with Gaudi 3 MMEs, for low-latency, cost-effective inferencing.
  • Mid-2026 — OnDemand Intel Technical Certification (Intel Business partner brief).

13Redington × AIREV · 22 July 2026

MEA distribution of OnDemand

Redington will distribute UAE-built AIREV OnDemand agentic AI across the Middle East and Africa — software-only, self-hosted or cloud-hosted — not a hardware unit-count.

Muhammad Khalid (F-L), AIREV founder, and Sayantan Dev (F-R), President of MEA at Redington, sign the agreement, in the presence of Dr Thani Al Zeyoudi (B-C), UAE Minister of Foreign Trade and Chairman of AIREV.

Source: Gulf News, 22 July 2026 — “Redington to distribute UAE-built AIREV OnDemand agentic AI platform across MEA.” Photograph reproduced from that article. Redington is a trademark of its owner.

  • Redington to distribute AIREV OnDemand agentic AI across MEA.
  • Three deployment models: software-only, self-hosted, and cloud-hosted.
  • Implementation services, custom agent development, and multi-tier technical support through the channel.
  • OnDemand optimised to run on Intel Core Ultra Series 3, with agents on the client device.
  • Signatories: Muhammad Khalid, AIREV founder, and Sayantan Dev, President of MEA at Redington.
  • Witnessed by H.E. Dr Thani Al Zeyoudi, UAE Minister of Foreign Trade and Chairman of AIREV.
